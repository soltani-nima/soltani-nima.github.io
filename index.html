03Research

Research

One program, five kinds of structure

Each line of work asks what structure a signal carries, and how a learning system should use it when outcomes are uncertain: the fundamentals behind a price, the relations among assets, the equation behind a field, the dynamics of cortex, the rules of a protocol.

Current focus · Computational neuroscience

Vision, action, and deception

Perception and action are usually studied apart, yet every reach is planned from what the eyes report, and every movement changes what is seen next. Deception strains that loop. An actor can shape a movement to hide its goal; an observer has to infer the goal from the movement alone. With Dr. Narjes Soltani at Shahid Beheshti University, I am framing three questions for MEG:

  1. How do visual perception and action planning shape each other while a movement is prepared and carried out?
  2. When an action is meant to deceive, how is the hidden intent encoded in the actor, and how is it perceived by an observer?
  3. Can that intent be decoded from MEG, from localized sources and their time–frequency dynamics, and how early?

MEG preprocessingSource localizationTime–frequency analysisNeural decoding

i

Markets

Reinforcement-learning agents for trading and portfolio allocation that combine price dynamics with company fundamentals and financial text. The backbones are transformers and xLSTM; a conservative on-policy diffusion policy is meant to keep portfolio behaviour stable.

ii

Structure

Graph and hypergraph neural networks in which co-movement, influence, and regime structure among assets are modelled directly, not appended as features after the fact.

iii

Physics

Physics-informed Kolmogorov–Arnold networks for forward and inverse PDE problems. In PG-KINN, a Petrov–Galerkin weak formulation is paired with the learnable univariate functions of a KAN.

iv

Brain signals

Pipelines for MEG preprocessing, source localization, and decoding, with spectral and time–frequency analysis of activity related to visual perception and cognition. This is the groundwork for the vision–action questions above.

v

Protocols

Ethereum protocol design, consensus mechanisms, Solidity contracts, and on-chain transaction modelling, studied through the security questions that cryptocurrency and DeFi infrastructure raise.

    Under review Applied Soft Computing · Elsevier Preprint on SSRN First author

    Automated Stock Trading Using Deep Reinforcement Learning and Fused xLSTM Networks

    N. Soltani, A. Sadr, A. Pakniyat, K. Pakzad, D. Rahmati

    Uses fused xLSTM networks as the memory of a PPO agent that makes sequential trading decisions in nonstationary markets.

    D. Rahmati, N. Soltani, A. Sadr

    Fuses technical and fundamental signals in a transformer-based PPO agent for portfolio trading, and trains its diffusion policy conservatively on-policy for robustness.
