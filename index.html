01About

About

Learning systems that must act before the evidence is in

I study Computer Engineering at Shahid Beheshti University and work as a research assistant at the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM) and at Shahid Beheshti University. My work asks how learning systems should decide when the signals they depend on are heterogeneous, structured, and incomplete.

At IPM, this means reinforcement-learning agents for trading and portfolio decisions that combine technical, fundamental, and textual inputs; graph and hypergraph models of market structure; and physics-informed Kolmogorov–Arnold networks for partial differential equations. The work has led to manuscripts under review at Elsevier journals and an arXiv preprint. At Shahid Beheshti University, I study Ethereum protocols and smart-contract security, and analyse MEG recordings, from preprocessing and source localization to neural decoding, to study visual perception and cognitive processing.

Separately, my current research focus is the intersection of vision and action, especially deception: how deceptive intent is expressed in an action and read by an observer. Alongside the research, I teach as an assistant at Sharif University of Technology, Amirkabir University of Technology, and Shahid Beheshti University, in courses from first-year programming to graduate level.

03Research

Research

One program, many kinds of structure

Each line of work asks what structure a signal carries, and how a learning system should use it when outcomes are uncertain: the fundamentals behind a price, the relations among assets, the equation behind a field, the dynamics of cortex, the rules of a protocol.

Current research focus · Neuroscience

Vision, action, and deception

Perception and action are usually studied apart, yet every reach is planned from what the eyes report, and every movement changes what is seen next. Deception strains that loop. An actor can shape a movement to hide its goal; an observer has to infer the goal from the movement alone. The questions I am working toward:

  1. How do visual perception and action planning shape each other while a movement is prepared and carried out?
  2. When an action is meant to deceive, how is the hidden intent expressed in the movement, and how is it read by an observer?
  3. What does an observer rely on to see through a deceptive action, and how early in the movement does the intent become readable?

i

Markets

Reinforcement-learning agents for trading and portfolio allocation that combine price dynamics with company fundamentals and financial text. The backbones are transformers and xLSTM; a conservative on-policy diffusion policy is meant to keep portfolio behaviour stable.

ii

Structure

Graph and hypergraph neural networks in which co-movement, influence, and regime structure among assets are modelled directly, not appended as features after the fact.

iii

Physics

Physics-informed Kolmogorov–Arnold networks for forward and inverse PDE problems. In PG-KINN, a Petrov–Galerkin weak formulation is paired with the learnable univariate functions of a KAN.

iv

Brain signals

Pipelines for MEG preprocessing, source localization, and decoding, with spectral and time–frequency analysis of activity related to visual perception and cognition.

v

Protocols

Ethereum protocol design, consensus mechanisms, Solidity contracts, and on-chain transaction modelling, studied through the security questions that cryptocurrency and DeFi infrastructure raise.

Papers and preprints

    Under review Applied Soft Computing · Elsevier Preprint on SSRN First author

    Automated Stock Trading Using Deep Reinforcement Learning and Fused xLSTM Networks

    N. Soltani, A. Sadr, A. Pakniyat, K. Pakzad, D. Rahmati

    Uses fused xLSTM networks as the memory of a PPO agent that makes sequential trading decisions in nonstationary markets.

    Robust Multimodal Portfolio Trading Using Transformer-Based PPO: Technical–Fundamental Fusion with Conservative On-Policy Diffusion

    N. Soltani, A. Sadr, D. Rahmati

    Fuses technical and fundamental signals in a transformer-based PPO agent for portfolio trading, and trains its diffusion policy conservatively on-policy for robustness.
